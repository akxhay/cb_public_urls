Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

xharma and the Chat bin app have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Chat bin does not permit content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual grooming of minors, sexual exploitation of children, sextortion involving minors, trafficking or solicitation of children for sexual purposes, and any other form of child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Prohibited Content and Behavior

Users must not use Chat bin to:

  • Create, upload, share, request, distribute, or promote CSAM.
  • Sexually exploit or abuse children.
  • Groom or solicit minors for sexual purposes.
  • Facilitate sexual contact or exploitation involving minors.
  • Promote or facilitate child trafficking or sexual exploitation.
  • Threaten, coerce, or blackmail a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Share sexual content involving minors or attempt to obtain such content.

Handling of Reports

We take reports concerning child safety seriously. When we become aware of suspected CSAE or CSAM, we may take appropriate action, including removing or restricting access to content, suspending or terminating accounts, preserving relevant information where appropriate, and reporting matters to relevant authorities or organizations when required by applicable law.

Cooperation With Authorities

xharma will cooperate with appropriate law-enforcement authorities and relevant organizations in accordance with applicable laws and legal requirements concerning child sexual abuse and exploitation.
