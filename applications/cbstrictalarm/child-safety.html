Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation

xharma and the CB Strict Alarm app have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

CB Strict Alarm does not permit content or behavior that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexual grooming of minors, sexual exploitation of children, sextortion involving minors, trafficking or solicitation of children for sexual purposes, and any other form of child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Prohibited Content and Behavior

Users must not use CB Strict Alarm to:

  • Create, upload, share, request, distribute, or promote CSAM.
  • Sexually exploit or abuse children.
  • Groom or solicit minors for sexual purposes.
  • Facilitate sexual contact or exploitation involving minors.
  • Promote or facilitate child trafficking or sexual exploitation.
  • Threaten, coerce, or blackmail a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Share sexual content involving minors or attempt to obtain such content.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation, or other child-safety concerns by contacting us at:

Child Safety Contact: akxhayxharma@gmail.com

Reports should include enough information for us to understand and investigate the concern, such as relevant usernames, content, file references, or other identifying information where available.

Handling of Reports

We take reports concerning child safety seriously. When we become aware of suspected CSAE or CSAM, we may take appropriate action, including removing or restricting access to content, suspending or terminating user access, preserving relevant information where appropriate, and reporting matters to relevant authorities or organizations when required by applicable law.

Cooperation With Authorities

xharma will cooperate with appropriate law-enforcement authorities and relevant organizations in accordance with applicable laws and legal requirements concerning child sexual abuse and exploitation.
